# Synthetic staff pulse survey

Each row is one distinct response. respondent_id is an opaque identifier; department and tenure are respondent attributes. No weighting or deduplication is needed. Empty cells are missing, including the one missing tenure value. Keep that tenure group as Unknown rather than dropping the response.

satisfaction is an integer from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied); 4 and 5 count as favorable. recommend is yes or no. Missingness is measure-specific: a respondent may answer one question and skip another.

This small synthetic exercise mimics a voluntary convenience sample. The number invited, nonrespondents' characteristics, and population composition are unavailable. It cannot establish a participation rate, a representative workforce estimate, change over time, statistical significance, or causal explanations. Segment membership is descriptive. The supplied rows are the only observations.
