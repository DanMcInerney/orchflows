# Acceptance

The delivered page meets the public content, semantics and navigation requirements. The domains can include code, visual design and writing; their coexistence is not proof that three separate candidate reviews are useful. Audit actual guidance selection and coverage, not just headings in a plan.

Apply the selected Dynamic contract. Do not demand an exact gate count or penalize an otherwise allowed intermediate review merely because this study hypothesizes one joined unit. Record review scopes, duplicated review of unchanged states, and cost as observations. Under a tested boundary-policy amendment, judge its stated requirements separately from useful output quality.

The objective checker covers required semantic content and the anchor. It cannot establish responsive visual quality, contrast, usability or actual browser play. Missing rendered evidence must limit those claims. Alternate visual treatments and wording are acceptable; an inert placeholder page is not.
