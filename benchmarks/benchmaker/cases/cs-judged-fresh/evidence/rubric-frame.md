# Rubric frame — meeting-minutes condenser

Criteria structure only. Anchors are NOT given here: the benchmark
builder authors them from `evidence/sources/`, never invents them.

## Criteria

- **coverage** — the minutes carry the decisions and owners the five
  meetings record, across every meeting.
- **no-invention** — the minutes state nothing the sources do not
  state; every claim cites the source line id it rests on, in square
  brackets, e.g. `[m1-02]`.
- **length bound** — the minutes body stays within 160 words, citation
  tokens excluded.

## Split law

Byte-decidable facts — citation resolution, the length bound,
per-source coverage — are deterministic criteria and form the required
floor. Judged renderings of coverage quality and faithfulness are
secondary criteria: they carry a stated scale with anchors drawn from
the sources, they never compensate for a deterministic failure, and
their judge rerun variance is recorded before the package seals.
