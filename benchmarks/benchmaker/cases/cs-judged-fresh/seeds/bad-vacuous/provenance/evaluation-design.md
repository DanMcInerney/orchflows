# Evaluation design excerpt — meeting-minutes condenser benchmark

Frozen before construction; candidate-blind.

- Outcome: the minutes digest one condenser variant produces from the
  five supplied meeting transcripts.
- Split: byte-decidable facts (citation resolution against source line
  ids, 160-word bound, per-source coverage) are required deterministic
  criteria; coverage quality and faithfulness are judged criteria,
  secondary and non-compensating.
- Anchors: authored from the supplied sources only; every anchor cites
  a source line id.
- Variance: three judge reruns per judged criterion; the spread is
  recorded in the qualification component before the seal.
- Selection rationale: criteria cover every rubric-frame clause; no
  criterion rests on material the evidence does not exhibit.
