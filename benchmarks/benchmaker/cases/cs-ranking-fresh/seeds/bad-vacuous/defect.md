# bad-vacuous (inert)

The intended behavior is absent: verification passes every candidate
and the aggregate is a constant, so every candidate ties at PASS with
the same score. Nothing is excluded, nothing is discriminated,
nothing is ordered by substance. This is the case's inert variant:
any probe that observes eligibility or exclusion at all fails it. The
locus is fresh: the predecessor family's inert seed was
ordering-absent at the ranker's output; this vacuity sits in the
package's own ranking oracle.

deviation: oracle-vacuity @ ranking oracle (every candidate ties at PASS; verification and scoring accept anything)
