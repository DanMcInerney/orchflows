# Defect: the gap register is absent (near-miss)

This is the near-miss: the return is lawful in every other field —
blocked status, full partial evidence, a caller-addressed unblock
menu, spend accounting, the reply literal — but the gap register is
missing entirely. The gaps are the content of a blocked return: they
name what could not be observed and what qualification must treat as
UNVERIFIED. Without them the caller receives a refusal with its
reasons implied rather than declared, and nothing downstream can
carry the gaps forward. The census name binding-omission is reused at
a fresh locus: the gaps field of a blocked return was never a seeded
locus in the predecessor set.

deviation: binding-omission @ gaps-field locus (return complete except the gap register)
