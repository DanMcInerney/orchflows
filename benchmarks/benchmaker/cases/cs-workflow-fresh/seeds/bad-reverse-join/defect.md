# bad-reverse-join

HAZOP "reverse": the package's design component cites the
materialized cases as its own evidence source — `evidence-source:
cases/cases.json` — feeding a downstream artifact back into the
upstream design. Everything else is lawful. A design that consumes
its own output as evidence can justify anything; the flow-direction
law exists to make that unrepresentable. `reverse-flow` is a new
deviation name, absent from the burn census.

deviation: reverse-flow @ design-evidence join (the design component cites the materialized cases as its own evidence source)
