# Support FAQ — order totals

**My ten-unit order came to 2475, not 2250. Why?**

Ten units at 250 is not "10% off the whole order". Nine units bill at
list (2250) and only the tenth unit sits in the volume band (225).
2250 + 225 = 2475.

**We ordered 50 units at 400 and were billed 18300. Is the bulk rate
broken?**

No. Only the fiftieth unit reaches the bulk band. Nine units at list
(3600), forty units at 10% off (14400), one unit at 25% off (300).

**Does a single unit ever get a discount?**

No. One unit at 250 is 250, and nine units at 250 is 2250 — the
starter band has no discount.

**Do we round up a half cent?**

No. Every band subtotal drops its fraction of a cent.
