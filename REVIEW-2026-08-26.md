## Meta-analysis

Three causes connect almost every thread: routing and lifecycle prose describe states no public producer materializes; pack schemas declare data without mechanically carrying it to consumers; and admission checks often recognize words or labels instead of the contract structure they claim to enforce. Duplication then hides those gaps by making multiple surfaces look authoritative while none owns the whole transition.

The single simplifying move with the widest reach is to make each public artifact transition a closed, script-owned typed operation, then delete every consumer paraphrase of that operation. That move closes routing/resume/terminal-record producer gaps (T1, T2, T6, T9), gives pack fields and evidence one carrier path (T3–T5, T8), turns repeated deterministic review into real admission (T10–T11), and enables the deletion wave in T12. The move is intentionally ordered: materialize the live producers and discriminating oracles first, then delete the claims and copies they supersede. No such correction is performed in this report.
