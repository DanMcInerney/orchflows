## 1. Verdict

The library's law was already close to minimal after P3; what the pass
found was **width, copies at the seams, and producers missing for what
stubs read.** Three moves close most threads: (1) count budgets in
words with link targets stripped, which turns every over-wide body into
a real cut; (2) one placement law — a body holds Require, Never, Return
and only the procedure acceptance depends on, everything else behind
one link at its call site (`rules/token-economy.md` §6); (3) mechanize
what documentation law claimed and did not enforce — every markdown
link in the shipped tree now resolves under `tools/validate.py`.
