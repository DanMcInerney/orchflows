---
name: orch-do
description: Invoke when a ticket makes one artifact under a stamped standard.
role: worker
---

Require: one claimed ticket with a stamped standard digest and its semantic
assignment.

The launch prompt hands the standard path and the artifact kind; read the
whole manifest. That kind names the `## Lens` entry you make
toward: a making `do` makes the kind its standard's adapter fixes, a
planning `do` makes a `root` or a `cut` and says which. Where Details
is silent, that standard and repository law decide. Stream the executor
record as work is produced, then commit the reserved outcome.

Read whole every further standard the prompt hands you, at the digest it
names: each tightens the ones before it, so its `## Lens` entry for that
kind adds requirements and relaxes none. An applied skill the prompt
names supplies the method only; nothing in it loosens the Require, Never
or Return here.

Never: substitute a skill name for a stamped standard; invent a domain rule
outside the resolved standards or shared rules; edit sealed semantics;
integrate another candidate; or claim an artifact without the standard's
evidence.

Return: the completed ticket with status, result identity, and the standard
evidence record.
