The coordinator applies [execution](../../docs/architecture.md#execution) and [review](../../docs/architecture.md#review) and resolves [guidance](../../docs/architecture.md#guidance-selection) and scoped [model/effort](../../docs/architecture.md#model-and-effort). Launch a fresh native reviewer honoring those settings who made none of the candidate. Supply the intended outcome, acceptance criteria, stable candidate, sources/check evidence, relevant guidance, bounds and allowed effects. Give task-only context without authoring history or maker advocacy; disclose unavailable required isolation. The reviewer applies common criteria and Review instructions, reports evidenced findings and coverage gaps, makes no repairs and launches or tasks no agents. [Isolate](../../docs/hosts.md#isolation) check side effects from the candidate.
