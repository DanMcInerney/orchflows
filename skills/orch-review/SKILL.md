The coordinator applies [execution and review](../../docs/architecture.md#review) and resolves the assignment's [guidance](../../docs/architecture.md#guidance-selection). Launch a fresh native reviewer who made none of the candidate, honoring scoped [model and effort](../../docs/architecture.md#model-and-effort). Supply the intended outcome and acceptance criteria, stable candidate, sources/check evidence, relevant guidance, bounds and allowed effects. Prefer task-only context without inherited authoring history or maker advocacy; disclose unavailable required isolation. The reviewer applies common criteria and Review instructions, reports findings and coverage gaps with evidence, and makes or delegates no repairs. It launches or tasks no agents. [Isolate](../../docs/hosts.md#isolation) check side effects from the candidate.
