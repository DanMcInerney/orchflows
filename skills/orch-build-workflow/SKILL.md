Name the recurring request and useful result. Apply [architecture](../../docs/architecture.md) and [library authoring](../../docs/libraries.md) with `orchflows` and `writing` guidance. Author directly when settings permit or use [orch-work](../orch-work/SKILL.md).

Save the process, inputs, outputs and stopping conditions. Keep task details in the prompt and quality criteria, methods and taste in guidance. Discover relevant components; supply their inputs without restating their contracts. Compose them in this coordinator at any depth. Ordinary production steps may leave staffing flexible; require a fresh maker only deliberately. Keep library context to dependencies, scoped guidance and requested saved settings. Verify [invocation policy](../../docs/hosts.md#invocation-policy).

Before review, [trial the behavior](../../docs/hosts.md#workflow-trials) on ordinary inputs in an unrelated workspace. Complete the trial and its required judgments before launching authoring review. Record preparation, interventions and gaps. Repair observed failures and rerun affected behavior as needed. Improvement claims require matched old/new trials.

Apply [orch-review-revise-once](../orch-review-revise-once/SKILL.md) to the candidate and trial evidence. Repeat affected trials when repairs require it. Return the artifact, evidence and gaps; verify native registration before claiming availability by name.
