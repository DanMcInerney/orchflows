# Sheet contract

A sheet is extra craft, stamped on one ticket beside its pack. The pack owns
the domain; a sheet narrows that domain for one assignment — a house style,
a client's report shape, a family of checks a run wants applied — without
forking the pack or editing the craft every other run reads.

It is a ring item and nothing else: resolved through
[scripts/rings.py](../scripts/rings.py)'s one order, digest-pinned on the
ticket at issue like a pack, and read at that digest by the ticket's maker
and by its judge. No host adapter is written for a sheet, because nothing
invokes one: it is stamped, never called.

## Location and anatomy

Ring directory `sheets`, library directory `sheets`, manifest `SHEET.md` —
so a sheet lives at `sheets/<name>/SHEET.md` in whichever ring carries it,
and the installer mints the same `by-name/<name>/SHEET.md` pointer it mints
for a pack. The `orch-` prefix is reserved here as it is for every other
kind.

A sheet directory carries prose and nothing executable. A `scripts`
directory, a `requirements.txt` and a `tools.txt` beside it are refused: a
sheet has no code of its own, so it declares no dependencies and owns no
environment.

## Frontmatter

- `name` — the sheet's name, equal to its directory name.
- `description` — one sentence, at most 140 characters, saying when to stamp
  it.
- `packs` — the pack names this sheet may be stamped beside. A ticket
  stamping it against any other pack is refused, because a sheet tightens a
  craft it was written against and says nothing about one it was not.

## Sections

- `## Craft` — required. What this sheet adds to the stamped pack's craft
  for the maker.
- `## Lens` — required. One `###` entry per artifact kind, keyed exactly as
  a craft's `## Lens` entries are keyed in
  [pack-signature.md](pack-signature.md): the adapter kinds `git`, `doc` and
  `evidence`. Each entry adds criteria beside the craft's entry of the same
  key, and every pack `packs` names has its adapter's kind among the
  entries — a stamp whose kind the sheet does not key adds nothing the
  ticket's verbs read.
- `## Vocabulary` — optional. Terms this sheet introduces, defined once.

`## Workspace`, `## Stages` and `## Spec fields` are refused. Those three are
the pack's: identities and isolation, the stage sequence, and what a spec
must carry are facts about a domain, and a sheet that restated one would be
a second owner of it.

## Additive and tighten-only

A sheet adds requirements and narrows the ones the craft already states. It
never loosens one. Where a sheet's clause would permit what the craft
forbids, the craft wins and the judge reports the conflict as a finding
tagged `sheet-defect` rather than following the looser text — the sheet is
the defect, not the artifact.

## Budget

At most 100 non-empty lines in the manifest. A sheet longer than that is
carrying a craft's worth of law and belongs in a pack, and the ceiling sits
beside the craft's for that reason.

## Identity

A sheet's digest is SHA-256 over its directory tree: every file's
repository-relative path, sorted, with its bytes. The ticket pins that digest
at issue and every later door re-derives it, so a sheet that changed under a
sealed assignment — or a nearer ring that came to shadow it — is a refusal
rather than a substitution. [work-item.md](work-item.md) owns the ticket
fields that carry the pin; [scripts/tickets_pins.py](../scripts/tickets_pins.py)
is the one resolver, hasher and verifier for them.
