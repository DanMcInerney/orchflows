# Research

Prefer primary sources that directly support important claims. Link evidence beside the claim. For time-sensitive claims, distinguish event, publication and substantive revision dates.

Keep observations, interpretations and recommendations distinguishable. Preserve qualifications and counterevidence that could change the conclusion. Report uncovered ground as a gap, not evidence of absence.

## Review

Trace central claims to their evidence. Flag conclusions stronger than their sources, recycled sources counted as independent, and omitted evidence that materially changes the answer.
