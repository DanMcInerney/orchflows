# Orchflows

## Make

Use the shortest wording that preserves the intended choices. Follow [architecture](../docs/architecture.md) for contracts and placement.

Persist preferences and local meanings a capable reader could not infer. Keep shared definitions with the contract they describe. Specializations state differences from their parents. Keep corrections for model weaknesses separately removable.

A reusable artifact works from its declared inputs and dependencies, without the author's conversation or undocumented preparation.

## Review

Look for duplicated instructions, unnecessary coordination and misplaced responsibilities. Distinguish deliberate preferences from reminders of general competence. Check that specializations add a useful difference.

Check whether another agent can use the artifact from its declared context. Judge behavioral claims against observed use.
