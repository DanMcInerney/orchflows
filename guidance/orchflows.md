# Orchflows

For composing processes and authoring workflows or guidance. Follow [architecture](../docs/architecture.md) for contracts and [libraries](../docs/libraries.md) for placement and packaging.

Preserve deliberate choices in the shortest clear wording. Define what the reader cannot infer; reference shared contracts. Judge concision across all required context, not just the entrypoint.

Group work whose quality should be judged together; split where downstream work needs a checked decision or artifact. Guidance informs boundaries; domains, roles and files do not determine gate counts. Keep coordination proportional to the work.

Expose expected variations as inputs without speculative options or dependence on authoring history. Preserve chosen processes; leave other staffing flexible. Reuse components whose contracts fit; plain assignments may be simpler.

## Guidance

Write reusable quality criteria, methods and taste for a recurring kind of work, usable without its original workflow. Reuse existing guidance unless useful differences justify another domain or specialization. Specializations state differences from parents, not the name of a consuming skill.

Put shared criteria in the common body. Use optional Make and Review sections for role-specific methods without duplicating those criteria. Keep task details in prompts, process obligations in workflows and supporting knowledge in references. Use subject guidance as source material when authoring it.

Keep temporary model corrections separately removable, each naming the failure it corrects and the model it was observed on.

## Review

For workflows, check coherent results, consequential handoffs, scoped guidance and settings, and clear inputs, outputs and stopping conditions. Flag duplicated instructions, unnecessary gates or specializations, misplaced responsibilities and dependence on authoring history.

Inspect actual consumer trials, resulting artifacts and native process evidence. Distinguish demonstrated behavior from predictions and untested branches. Preserve deliberate choices; cut instructions that add no useful distinction.
