`orchflows sync` reads each item's `tools.txt` (grammar in §2) and reports every missing tool or variable with its line and installs nothing; runs the lockfile install for an item's `package.json` into the item's `node_modules/` when the item is trusted and `node` resolves, else reports the remedy; removes `~/.orchflows/envs/<kind>/<name>/` for items no longer in the inventory; the ring `.gitignore` block and the block `sync --project` writes both carry `node_modules/`; `orchflows check` validates `tools.txt` grammar; `orchflows env <kind> <name>` is unchanged.
