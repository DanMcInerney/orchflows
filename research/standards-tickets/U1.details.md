Unit U1: resolution and pinning

Spec: research/standards-spec-2026-09-04.md. Read section 0 (decisions 2, 3 and 6 are this unit), section 2, and section 3 U1. Decisions are closed: where one looks wrong, report the observation in `## Report` and continue.

Why: a sheet today declares `packs: [<names>]` and is stamped flat — one level, no depth. The cascade the spec buys is `narrows:`, a single parent walked to a root, so `three-js` narrows `javascript` narrows `code` and the worker reads all three. Everything else about a sheet already works: it is digest-pinned at issue, re-derived at every door, read at that digest by the maker and by the judge, and tighten-only. This unit adds the walk and nothing else.

Change, the walk: given a stamped name, resolve it to a chain by following `narrows:` to a standard that carries none. Refuse a cycle by name, a chain that has not terminated in eight hops, and a parent that resolves in no reachable ring. Check the resolved set carries exactly one `adapter`: zero and the ticket has no workspace mechanism, two and it has a contradiction.

Change, the pin: the ticket carries the whole chain as an ordered list of `(name, digest)`, broad to narrow, in place of today's `pack`, `pack_digest` and `sheets`. The stamping flag is repeatable; each name resolves its own chain; chains concatenate in the order written; a standard reached twice is read once, at its first position, so a shared ancestor is not read twice.

The files: `tickets_pins.py` (the pin), `packs.py` and `packs_support.py` (resolution), `tickets_stamp_generation.py` and `tickets_generations.py` (the frontmatter the assignment seal covers — the seal now covers `standards` where it covered three fields), and `tickets_commands.py`, `tickets_mint.py` and `tickets_issue.py` for the flag.

Work under the OLD directory names. Items still live in `packs/` and `sheets/` when this unit lands; U2 changes their content and U3 moves them. Adding a `narrows:` field to a sheet so a test has something to walk is yours to add and U2's to keep. Do not rename a module, a flag, a directory or a finding code — that is U3's, and a rename racing this change is the collision the wave order exists to avoid.

New checks, each with its failing reading recorded before the production change: a cycle refuses; a ninth hop refuses; a missing parent refuses; a resolved set with two adapters refuses; a set with none refuses; a chain of three pins three digests in broad-to-narrow order; a standard named twice is read once.
