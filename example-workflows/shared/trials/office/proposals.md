# Supplier proposals and decision criteria

Fictional planning records. Prices are in units. Choose a service ready to support CSV export, onboarding within five business days, and a first-year total no greater than 10,000 units. Compare twelve monthly charges plus all onboarding fees. An unconfirmed required capability is a gap, not a pass. Prefer lower total cost among proposals meeting the requirements; do not place an order.
