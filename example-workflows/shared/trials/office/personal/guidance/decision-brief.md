# Decision brief

## Make

Lead with the recommendation and its material condition. Show comparable first-year totals including one-time charges. Keep facts traceable to quotation identifiers and distinguish unconfirmed capabilities from failed ones. Keep the delivered memo within 220 words; supporting evidence can be separate. Recommend only; do not imply a purchase or approval occurred.

## Review

Check every quoted price, total and capability against the supplied sources and selected criteria. Identify missing evidence and unsupported completion claims. Keep a recommendation conditional when an eligibility condition remains unresolved.
