# Fictional operations source packet
Question: which approach should a 12-person service team pilot to reduce weekly reporting work without exposing client data?
## Team record OPS-1, 2026-09-14
The team spends 6 hours per week assembling status summaries. Reports contain client identifiers. Staff can approve a four-week internal pilot; no new external vendor upload is authorized. The existing spreadsheet and local Python are available.
## Option sheet OPT-1
Template-only: 1 hour setup, no recurring fee, no measured time saving. It retains manual copying.
Local CSV summarizer: 8 hours estimated setup; runs on company laptops; zero license fee; exports a summary from an approved local CSV. It must preserve source rows, flag missing status and avoid repeating client identifiers in shared output. There is no measured saving yet.
Hosted assistant: advertised 4 hours saved weekly, unverified; 20 units per person monthly, annual billing; client data sent to provider. Security review and export compatibility are unverified.
## Pilot measurement note MEAS-1
Compare preparation minutes and erroneous/missing summaries against the same week's current process. Use synthetic or approved local records. Report observed results, not estimated savings as fact. Source sheets are planning evidence, not a completed trial.
