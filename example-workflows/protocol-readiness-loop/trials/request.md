# Forward trial

From an unrelated workspace, invoke `protocol-readiness-loop:protocol-readiness-loop` on a draft experiment protocol with a round limit of 3 and conformance-repair limit of 2. Use fresh role contexts and preserve every protocol version.

Exercise four bounded cases separately: ordinary convergence after one accepted revision; one evidenced failure class recurring after two revisions; an accepted finding whose exact executable semantics require a separately authorized mechanism probe; and conformance that still fails after both repair attempts. Do not build a probe, harness or experiment artifact. Preserve the ledgers and validate each with the bundled script.
