# Trial acceptance

Evaluate the output directory, the commands actually run and native events. Record the candidate revision, interpreter, dates, elapsed time and interventions, including any interruption.

- The skill acquires in the invoking context and launches no agents. Scripts resolve from the skill directory; the plan and evidence stay in the supplied directory, outside the package.
- One plan carries the requested window and limits; its worst-case steps and records fit them. Discovery uses Hacker News routes and pauses for selection.
- Choices name exact record and depth IDs from the saved candidates, with reasons drawn from retained text and context and an omission reason. At most two discussions are read; choosing none is acceptable when justified.
- Depth reads cover only the chosen discussions. Recorded requests, records and active time stay within the limits. Refusals or rate limits appear as gaps, without retries or substitute sources.
- Resume with the unchanged plan and output makes no further requests for completed steps and leaves the packet unchanged. If an interruption was exercised, the uncertain read is not replayed, its reservation remains and the gap is reported.
- The return separates inspected evidence and coverage gaps from conclusions. A valid packet is not presented as an answer or complete coverage; reported dates stay distinct from verified publication dates.
