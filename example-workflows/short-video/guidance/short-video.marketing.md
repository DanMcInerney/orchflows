# Marketing video

## Make

Connect a recognizable audience need to a specific product benefit and credible support. Make the product integral to the memorable image or demonstration; a slogan alone cannot establish value. Make illustrations, demonstrations, testimonials and measured results distinguishable. Close in a way that serves the campaign's intent; a call to action earns its place rather than being compulsory.

Use references to understand useful choices, not to inherit a competitor's promises, look or sequence. Keep performance claims within the supplied evidence; popularity and creator enthusiasm do not prove conversion or causal effectiveness.

## Review

Can this audience connect the promised benefit to what the film actually shows? Check visible and implied claims, numerical evidence and the closing invitation against current product facts and the brief. Identify borrowed expression or unsupported results. Assess whether the product is understandable and the persuasion earned, rather than scoring compliance with a universal ad formula.
