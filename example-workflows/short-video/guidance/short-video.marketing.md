# Marketing video

## Make

Connect an audience need to a specific product benefit and credible support. Integrate the product into the memorable image/demonstration; slogans alone prove no value. Distinguish illustrations, demonstrations, testimonials and measured results. Close for the campaign's intent; use a CTA only when earned.

Learn from references without inheriting competitors' claims, look or sequence. Bound performance claims by evidence; popularity/enthusiasm prove neither conversion nor causality.

## Review

Can the audience connect the promised benefit to what is shown? Check visible/implied claims, numbers and closing invitation against current product facts and the brief. Flag copied expression and unsupported results. Judge product clarity and earned persuasion, not a universal ad formula.
