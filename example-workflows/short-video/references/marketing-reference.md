# Marketing source observations

Read 2026-09-11. These are optional source context, not a research stage or a template.

[8StarLabs' Remotion and Claude account](https://www.8starlabs.com/blogs/remotion-claude), published 2026-03-29, describes a component showcase built around the product itself, shared time constants, beat-aligned transitions, readable introductions and seeded procedural variation. The author reports these production choices; the article supplies no controlled conversion evidence. The transferable possibility is to make a product's behavior visually legible through repeatable timing. A soundtrack, component-card layout and CTA are choices from that case.

[TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads) recommends a clear opening, developed message and close, platform-aware framing and purposeful sound for TikTok ads. That advertising context does not establish universal requirements for personal films, jokes, education, silence or other placements. Applying either source to a new brief is a design hypothesis to judge in the resulting film.
