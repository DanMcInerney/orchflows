---
name: render-video
description: Apply when making a locked portrait video project from approved direction and assets.
role: worker
---

Require: approved script/storyboard, duration, brand, asset rights and production
settings; the rendering project's pinned standard and delivery requirements.

Before making, read [renderer](../../references/renderer.md) for the qualified
scaffold, local asset/voice route and external probe. Build one thin render
through that boundary before expanding the film. Derive motion and caption
display from frames and measured phrase durations. Preserve input and tool
provenance with the runnable source and inspect actual rendered playback.

Never: orchestrate other calls; invent word times, heard quality or license
eligibility; substitute technical metadata for audiovisual judgment.

Return: artifact identity and evidence in the executor result, render/preview
paths, timing and asset provenance, probe exits, and unexercised branches
(`[]` when none), including partial output on failure.
