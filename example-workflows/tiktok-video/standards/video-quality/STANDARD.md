# Video quality

A production brief fixes duration, audience, message, brand, accepted direction,
renderer identity, technical targets and permitted assets/providers. Evaluation
permission and production license eligibility are distinct evidence. Unknown
eligibility is unresolved; a valid evaluation render does not settle it.

## Making

A thin rendered beat proves the chosen visual, caption and sound language before
expansion. The film communicates an original, supportable message without copying
reference scripts, layouts or assets. Structure fits the actual duration: one
readable idea can suffice at one second; two minutes need explanatory progression
and measured pauses, not stretched cards. Claims and call to action stay faithful
to the accepted document; intentional omissions or deviations need evidence.

Motion serves comprehension. Preserve recognizable subject identity, spatial
relationships and readable current focus through transitions and camera changes.
Graph zooms hold meaningful scales long enough to read and expose composition;
a wide overview need not make every background label readable, but it cannot
replace readable explanation. Graphs, uninterrupted zooms, palettes, effects,
fixed shot counts and a particular demo's imagery are not universal requirements.

Typography has a clear hierarchy, adequate contrast and readable holds at intended
phone display size. Captions and essential labels avoid crop/UI-safe areas defined
by the brief; competing overlays cannot obscure the message. The muted film
remains understandable. Captions match the approved spoken script, use measured
phrase/audio timing and preserve intentional pauses. Word highlighting requires
qualified alignment evidence, never uniformly divided phrase times.

## Evidence and review

A reviewer identifies the fixed source revision and rendered file hash. Review
actual exported playback across its full duration, including every transition,
camera movement and final hold; record player, timestamps and observed defects.
Sample beginning, semantic beats, transition midpoints and end at delivery size
for typography, clipping and focus. Stills/contact sheets supplement motion review;
render completion, screenshots, frame counts and JSON cannot establish smooth
motion or audiovisual quality. Preview and exported-file discrepancies matter.

Compare accepted script, supplied caption text and visible burned captions at
recorded positions. Inspect continuity, timing and comprehension in context;
metadata-only caption checks do not establish what the viewer sees. Technical
endpoint fixtures are not evidence of creative quality at unproduced durations.

Voice and music carry source, license/permission, asset hashes and provider/model
identity; retain stock-voice provenance and original music authorship. Cloning,
paid providers and publication require separate explicit authorization. The mix
keeps voice foreground with deliberate fades/ducking and no abrupt truncation.
A listening-capable reviewer must hear the final mix and judge intelligibility,
pronunciation, completeness, musical fit and perceived caption/audio sync, citing
positions and an audition identity. Independent transcript comparison, decoded
signal/loudness and measured timing are useful separate evidence; none proves
hearing. Unavailable listening leaves those audible criteria explicitly unresolved
with a playable audition and a human-listening route, never an automatic pass.
A claimed human verdict names who heard which exact file and what they assessed.

Delivery defaults are 1080x1920, 30fps, H264/yuv420p, limited-range BT.709 primaries,
transfer and matrix, AAC 48kHz when audio is required, -16 LUFS +/-2 and true peak
at most -1 dBTP. Approved alternative audio targets are explicit. Duration matches
the brief within one video frame; codec padding tolerance is not narration drift.
Decode the entire export; absent, corrupt, wrong-size or wrong-duration output is
a defect. An explicitly approved silent video may omit audio; present audio still
meets its technical targets, and intentional silence cannot mask missing narration.

The fixed project includes locked runnable source, asset/timing provenance,
reproducible render and local preview instructions, final MP4, caption timeline,
cover/contact sheet and playable audition where applicable. Evidence names command
exits, probe settings, source/output identities, direct observations and uncovered
criteria. Assets too large to commit have immutable retrievable identities and
verified restoration instructions; a mutable local path alone is not fixed output.
