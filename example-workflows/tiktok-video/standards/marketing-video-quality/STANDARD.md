# Marketing realization

The rendered film makes the accepted offer, audience relevance and value
comprehensible at intended display size. Product and brand remain connected to
the benefit; polished decoration cannot substitute for that connection. A thin
beat pairs the principal value with its visible explanation or support before
the full film develops the accepted direction.

Inspect the actual claim and supporting visual together at identified playback
positions. Cropping, juxtaposition, emphasis and animation must not imply more
than the support warrants or conceal a material qualification. Demonstrations,
comparisons, results and testimonials retain the distinctions accepted in the
direction. Count-up intermediate values are visibly transitional; the supported
final quantity, unit and relevant context settle long enough to read.

Review focal order at phone size: the main idea wins attention and subsidiary
evidence remains available without competing with it. Across full playback,
transitions hand attention to the next meaningful subject, and dense passages
receive enough settled reading time and relief. Changing shot families preserve
recognizable product identity and the meaning of recurring visual cues. Evidence
includes positions where the hierarchy or handoff fails, not only hero stills.

The close delivers the opening promise with an earned product/brand connection
and response suited to the brief, including awareness when intended. Judge these
relationships against the accepted direction; no palette, mockup, metric quota,
testimonial, transition effect, shot count or copied reference arc is required.
