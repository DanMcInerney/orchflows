# Marketing direction

The direction makes the offer, intended audience and specific value identifiable.
Its promise addresses an audience need without inventing a pain point. Brand and
product participate in the benefit or explanation, rather than appearing only as
an interchangeable end logo. Awareness can be the intended outcome; a purchase
request is not compulsory.

The smallest useful slice pairs one value proposition with a visual that makes
it understandable. The whole film earns its promise through an appropriate
mechanism, demonstration or support within the actual duration. Proof means
support suited to a claim: an observable artifact, demonstration, comparison,
verified result or attributed testimony may serve different claims. The plan
distinguishes what each supports from what it cannot establish. A metric or
testimonial quota is not proof; absent evidence narrows the promise.

Progression changes the viewer's understanding rather than accumulating slogans.
The closing payoff connects the opening promise, product and intended response.
No fixed problem/solution/results arc or number of beats is required. At very
short durations, a single concrete product benefit can carry that connection.

Each composition assigns a primary focal point and subordinate context. Type
scale, negative space and a consistent visual grammar distinguish the promise,
support and qualification. The storyboard budgets readable settled holds and
relief after dense beats; motion reveals information or transfers attention.
Varied shot families retain product identity and semantic continuity. Layouts
are composed for portrait viewing rather than blindly cropped from landscape.

Review the offer/audience/value inference, each claim's support and limits, the
planned focal order at phone size, the jobs of transitions and holds, and whether
the payoff is earned. Optional techniques such as count-ups, mockups, footage,
portraits or sparse bridge cards need a brief-specific communication purpose.
For count-ups, distinguish transitional values from the supported final quantity
and reserve a readable final hold. A reference's palette, slogans, scene order,
metrics and signature effects supply no recurring quality requirement.
