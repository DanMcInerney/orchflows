# Evolve

Base improvement claims on observed outputs under stable task-specific evaluation. Preserve caller purpose and required behavior through tradeoffs. Distinguish measurements, judge preferences and untested possibilities. Failures should yield a lesson, not an invented score.

## Make

Start with available task skills and a simple revision policy. Prefer one concrete hypothesis. Revisit promising alternatives or combine complementary ideas; tournaments should test distinct hypotheses. Retry failed ideas only with new evidence.

Record what was tried, what happened and what to try next. Carry relevant evidence rather than full transcripts. Retain a small archive of distinct approaches beside the original, current best and previous best.

Trace harness proposals to recorded failures; prefer one minimal change in prompts, tools, memory selection, verification or search whose effect is observable in outputs. Better-looking instructions establish no gain.

Reserve budget for comparison, required judges, confirmation and persistence before choosing width/repetitions. Favor confirmable experiments over unconfirmed candidates. Run independent candidates concurrently when resources permit; serialize measurements when contention distorts them. Screen cheaply before confirmation.

Methods cannot relax the workflow's bounds, independence, versioning, promotion or continuation.
