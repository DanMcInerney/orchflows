Evaluation starts from the library's trials. Synthesized cases fill the uses they leave uncovered, including at least one that could reveal a quality loss the existing case cannot, and reserved confirmation cases are withheld from the maker, with any exposure recorded. The evaluation, its margin and decision rule are saved, and the seed is repeated under matched conditions, before any challenger result exists. Journal order matches the native event order. Where scoring can vary, identical outputs are re-scored. The run compares the variation with its cost margin and with the regressions its quality guard must catch. When noise approaches a margin within the attempt budget, it adds repeats or cases, or records the smallest detectable difference. A seed near the quality ceiling needs no change of goal, since cost is the stated goal, but a guard that passes everything cannot establish matched quality.

Cost comes from observed usage records, not estimates. The challenger is promoted only with lower cost beyond noise, no quality or requirement regression and a confirmed result on reserved cases; otherwise the seed stays. Composing trials run in fresh top-level sessions launched by the evolve coordinator. The attempt and time budgets hold. When the search ends, new reusable cases are added as new folders in the supplied copy's trial layout; the copy's existing files stay unchanged. The result reports saved case paths, or that none were made, with the best artifact, observed cost and stop reason.
