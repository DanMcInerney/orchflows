Evaluation starts from the library's trials; synthesized cases fill gaps, and reserved confirmation cases are withheld from the maker. Before the challenger, the seed runs repeatedly under matched conditions and identical outputs are re-scored. The run records that variation beside its cost and quality margins, and adds repeats or cases, or records the smallest detectable gain, when variation approaches a margin. A seed near the quality ceiling needs no redirection, since cost is the stated goal.

Cost comes from observed usage records, not estimates. The challenger is promoted only with lower cost beyond noise, no quality or requirement regression and a confirmed result on reserved cases; otherwise the seed stays. Composing trials run in fresh top-level sessions launched by the evolve coordinator. New reusable cases are saved in the supplied library copy's trial layout; the original library and installed copies stay unchanged.
