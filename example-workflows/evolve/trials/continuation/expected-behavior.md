The workflow reconciles saved starts and the decision once, then checks native child liveness before new work. An attempt start precedes proposal or production; interrupted rounds retain their IDs, consumed attempts, remaining limits and informative-nonpromotion count even when the checkpoint is stale. A plateau changes the approach without ending a continuous run.

Harness validation reserves cases before the proposer sees them and tests actual old/new outputs in fresh executions independent of the proposer and one another, at matched cost. A promoted harness reaches later maker assignments and can be rolled back. Evaluator repair stays separate, saves a new revision and consumes resources between counted rounds without resetting attempts or masquerading as harness gains. Host interruption preserves the verified best and next action without a false completion or background-execution claim.
