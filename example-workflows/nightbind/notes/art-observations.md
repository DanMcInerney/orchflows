# Caller observations for the art handoff

Prepared while the core is frozen for independent review. These observations inform step 12; they are not the completed asset contract or permission to bypass the core checkpoint. The game maker should choose the final art direction and dimensional/export contract from the accepted core.

- The corrected core camera uses orthographic height 18 and enlarged blockouts. Inspect every creature at the actual 1280×720 game view, including a dense wave. Use different primary silhouettes, posture, appendages and value groups for Emberling, Thornback, Voltwing, Mireling, Fangling and Mourning Moth.
- First and second evolutions should have deliberately authored forms that communicate both their lineage and changed ability. The placeholder currently reuses shape families; final Storm Serpent/Worldcoil, for example, should read as designed creatures rather than enlarged base wings.
- Capture cards and the six-slot HUD should help connect a creature seen in the arena with its name and role. The maker can choose small rendered portraits or another consistent visual treatment; text and colored sigils alone should not carry all identity.
- Ally attacks need a clear visual source. Current blockout beams often begin at the keeper even when an ally supplies the ability. During integration, make the companion's orientation/action and attack origin readable while preserving simulation ownership, hit timing and existing collider rules.
- The arena blockout is a circular floor and repeated pillars. Add a coherent authored environment kit and a few recognizable landmarks, with restraint near active movement/capture areas. Preserve the accepted traversal/collision contract unless a gameplay change is explicitly recorded and retested.
- Preserve important poison/charge/boss warnings beneath large evolved forms and effects. Player, allies, hostiles and warnings need distinct visual treatment in a crowded frame. Stronger transformation spectacle should still leave the next movement decision visible.
- Actual tested rendering uses ANGLE on Intel Graphics. Plan efficient repeated meshes/materials and inspect GLBs through the game's renderer settings. Runtime measurements decide budgets; inspect readability in both Blender and the game.

Required source/export deliverables come from the workflow's Blender contract: editable `.blend`, reproducible authoring/export settings or scripts, runtime GLBs and dependencies, metadata with dimensions/pivots/clips/costs, and inspected source plus game-camera views. Develop the final models after the core checkpoint and asset brief, using authored forms suited to their gameplay roles.
