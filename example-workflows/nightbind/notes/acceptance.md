# Acceptance observations for the requested game

These observations interpret the user brief; they do not prescribe the maker's recipes or combat equations. Separate natural play from seeded diagnostic setups in reports.

| Promise | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- |
| One run with 12 waves | Start through wave progression to a boss outcome, with the same squad and resources persisting across waves; clean retry afterward. |
| Slowly earned capture opportunity | Charge visibly advances with live time, one successful capture consumes the resource, and another attempt cannot bypass the cooldown. Pausing does not earn captures. |
| Capture an actual enemy as an ally | A player chooses among living enemies; the chosen enemy leaves the hostile population and the resulting companion's distinct ability affects ordinary combat. |
| Meaningful capture decisions | At least two compositions handle different threats differently; an observed tradeoff explains why taking an immediately useful monster may delay a planned evolution. Repeatedly grabbing an arbitrary target should not erase the intended planning advantage. Challenge whether useful composition decisions remain after the initial squad fills, rather than assuming every recharge is meaningful. |
| Interesting, varied enemies | Distinct silhouettes, movement/attack patterns and warnings remain readable at the game camera. Show several roles interacting in a busy wave, not a catalog screen alone. |
| Hordes and rising pressure | Busy action has a substantial surrounding hostile population. Record counts, spatial pressure, damage/escape decisions and a gameplay image together; numerical spawn counts alone are insufficient. |
| Two earned power spikes | Ordinary sensible capture choices reach a visible first transformation around wave 5 and a second around wave 10. Each changes actual combat capability and creates a discernible release of pressure. Wrong/missing combinations delay or prevent the reward with understandable feedback. |
| Recipe understanding and recovery | Public instructions teach useful combinations and their timing before the opportunity is lost. A mistake can be identified and a sensible next capture pursued. |
| Final boss | The last wave has an identifiable boss with readable attacks and a meaningful culmination of squad choices; ordinary controls can reach victory and defeat. |
| Art supports play | Integrated Blender models distinguish enemy and ally roles, evolution tiers, the capture device and the boss at normal camera scale. Source/editability and exports are inspectable. |
| Agent can actually play | Supported browser controls can start, move continuously, choose captures, respond to threats, pause and retry. Diagnostics improve reproducibility without replacing ordinary input. |

For the two spike experiments, record pre/post hostile pressure and ally effects alongside the player's observed choices. Stronger damage alone does not establish a satisfying turnaround, and a screenshot of an evolved model alone does not establish an earned upgrade.

Desktop browser is the target. Report the actual graphics backend and observed busy-play frame timing. No mobile, controller or audible-quality claim is implied without checking it.
