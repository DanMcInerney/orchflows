# Core gameplay rubric

This rubric is the independent scorecard for the fixed I2 candidate. It
protects a gameplay-first gate: graybox geometry and functional placeholders
are allowed when the mechanic remains legible and fair.

Every hard gate is pass, fail, or unverified: production-like boot; focus and
ordinary controls; fundamental loop; every central mechanic and reachable
state; readable damage, timing, telegraphs, and transitions; loss, win, or
continuing state; restart and re-entry; complete core captures; and qualified
representative performance. A failed or unverified hard gate blocks.

Score each dimension from 0 to 4 with evidence IDs and a causal rationale:

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Loop and fun | absent or broken | playable with weak repetition or payoff | clear loop creates continued purposeful play |
| Challenge and counterplay | arbitrary or unfair | readable in places, uneven pressure | escalating pressure has learnable responses and relief |
| Choices and progression | no meaningful choice | choices work but collapse into one path | choices change tactics and sustain the session |
| Feedback and readability | actions cannot be read | major actions readable with ambiguity | timing, damage, telegraphs, and rewards are immediate and clear |
| Prompt fidelity | central promise missing | promise partly reachable | every core promise is reachable through ordinary input |

Scores never waive hard gates. Complaints classify defect, unverified claim,
or preference and name the seam, cause, evidence, expected improvement, and
regression check. A core pass freezes mechanics, control, collision, camera,
and timing for production art.
