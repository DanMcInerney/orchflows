# Core gameplay rubric

This rubric is the independent scorecard for the fixed I2 candidate. It
protects a gameplay-first gate: graybox geometry and functional placeholders
are allowed when the mechanic remains legible and fair.

Every hard gate is pass, fail, or unverified. The frozen hard-gate identities
are `production-boot`, `focus-controls`, `fundamental-loop`,
`central-mechanics-state-transitions`, `core-progression-content-paths`,
`terminal-continuing-behavior`, `replay-reentry`,
`readable-feedback-camera-collision`, `no-undisclosed-placeholder`,
`complete-core-captures`, and `qualified-representative-performance`.
`functional-final-boss` is also required when the brief marks a final boss as
promised. A failed or unverified hard gate blocks.

Score each dimension from 0 to 4 with evidence IDs and a causal rationale:

| Frozen dimension ID | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `loop-purpose-clarity` | purpose and loop are absent or broken | the loop is playable but repetition or payoff is weak | the purpose is clear and the loop creates continued purposeful play |
| `controls-camera-feel` | controls or camera prevent reliable play | control and camera response is usable but uneven | ordinary controls and camera feel precise, recoverable, and learnable |
| `interaction-feedback-readability` | actions, timing, or state changes cannot be read | major actions are readable with ambiguity | action, damage, telegraph, reward, and transition feedback is immediate and clear |
| `fairness-challenge` | pressure is arbitrary or unfair | pressure is readable in places with uneven counterplay | escalating pressure has learnable responses, relief, and fair counterplay |
| `meaningful-choice-progression` | no meaningful choice or progression exists | choices work but collapse into one path | choices change tactics and sustain the session |
| `pacing-continued-engagement` | pacing stops play or has no continued purpose | pacing supports a short session with weak escalation | pacing creates rising stakes, relief, and continued engagement |
| `prompt-fidelity` | a central promise is missing | a promise is partly reachable | every core promise is reachable through ordinary input |
| `stability` | blocking errors, stalls, or unrecoverable states prevent play | the session completes with material instability | the fixed candidate is stable, recoverable, and free of blocking errors |

Every applicable dimension has a documented floor of 3 on the 0–4 scale;
anchors and evidence must be recorded for each score. Scores never waive hard
gates. Complaints classify defect, unverified claim,
or preference and name the seam, cause, evidence, expected improvement, and
regression check. A core pass freezes mechanics, control, collision, camera,
and timing for production art.
