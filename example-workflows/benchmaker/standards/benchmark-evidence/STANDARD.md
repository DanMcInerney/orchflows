# Benchmark evidence

A benchmark research result defines one measured capability and its boundaries,
with enough licensed evidence to design falsifiable cases. The smallest useful
slice connects a claim, its falsifying observation and a case specification;
unsupported or uncased claims remain explicit gaps rather than implied coverage.

Target evidence covers stated claims, demand and failure records, boundaries
and refusals, the operator/harness through which outcomes are observed, and
relevant change history. Missing sources narrow the supported construct, not
the declared source policy. Field evidence identifies prior suites, comparable
artifacts/failures, failure taxonomies, oracle precedent and failure modes,
and gaming/contamination history. Each prior-art item has an adapt,
differentiate or ignore disposition with evidence and a reason. An empty field
finding carries the search paths and bounds that support it.

The synthesis fixes a construct definition; a claim register with source and
falsifying observation per claim; claim-to-case or claim-to-gap mappings; a
failure atlas distinguishing observed from taxonomy-derived modes and naming
the deviation each implies; prior-art dispositions; disagreement register and gap
register; and sourcing mode (mined, authored, generated-then-filtered) justified
by admissible evidence. Concrete exhibited inputs, outputs and traces are
oracle anchors or have a recorded reason they cannot be cased. Interpretation
never silently becomes a source observation.

Case provenance distinguishes original source, license/access restrictions,
authored changes, filtering and generator/seed identity where relevant. Publicly
exhibited material is assumed seen by candidates; it cannot become protected
material unchanged. A protected variant carries its authored deviation and
provenance, while actual access protection remains an independent empirical
finding. Off-tree placement and secrecy claims are not protection evidence.

Coverage maps distinguish supported claims, actual strata/cases and gaps.
A convenience sample is not a representative population. Difficulty claims
name the target configuration, sampling population, measurement date and
uncertainty evidence; generic benchmark principles cannot establish a numeric
pass-rate band. The default 30–50% band is a design requirement, not attributed
to literature. Historic controls establish only their observed harness behavior.

Review looks for unsupported construct expansion, missing falsifiers, unlicensed
case material, concealed sourcing/filtering, unreported prior-art alternatives,
misrepresented disagreement, exhibited answers mislabeled protected, and
confidence beyond evidence. Source denial and diminishing new failure classes
are documented acquisition limits; unresolved parts remain explicit gaps.
