# Benchmark quality

## Instrument validity

Validity (measuring the intended outcome) and target difficulty are separate.
Direction and final cases/outputs remain comparable to the original outcome
and subject facts, including relationships and interaction behavior where
relevant. Task-critical modalities need observed representative input evidence;
missing listening, ordinary-input or native observation capability is a gap,
not satisfied by tool presence.
The smallest useful slice contains visible case contracts, independent outcome
checks, reference correctness, controls and attributable target evidence.
Every case has a stable id, construct/failure mapping, provenance, stratum,
execution tier/cost, visible contract, reference, oracle class/required criteria,
split and revision ancestry. Coverage strata, floors and weights precede scores;
uncovered claims are gaps. One case per stratum is smoke coverage, not precise
stratum inference. Difficulty changes preserve strata, floors and weights.

Qualification separately evidences oracle failability, coverage, functional
discrimination, reproducibility, redundancy, provenance and cost. Known-good
references pass; inert and meaningful near-miss controls fail on observable
outcome changes. Equivalent bad variants are excluded with reasons; equivalent
inert behavior exposes an unobserved construct. Missing meaningful bad controls
leave discrimination unverified. Nondeterministic controls carry a declared
trial count and separate on every trial. Controls are never target measurements,
regardless of their record label or apparent score.

Deterministic outcome checks take priority; required deterministic failure
cannot be compensated by partial/judged credit. Judged criteria have anchored
rubrics, independent disagreement evidence and measured rerun spread. Missing
oracle evidence is UNVERIFIED. Licensed exhibited input/output/trace anchors
an outcome or has an explicit reason it cannot be cased; implementation origin
alone is no reason. Runnable component locators resolve before qualification.

The reference auditor is disjoint from builders and controls qualifier; the
attacker is a distinct context. The auditor solves from visible prompts and
licensed inputs before comparing keys/graders. All flagged all-fail, inversion
and ambiguous cases plus a predeclared remainder sample are covered; tiny
admissions audit every case. Fatal-flaw counts name cases and classes:
ambiguous, wrong-key, unsolvable, grader/contract-mismatch. Neither misses nor
successes alone establish reference correctness. Different model families are
recorded when available, never invented.

## Empirical evidence

Pin target artifact, actual model/version availability, effort, host/CLI,
scaffold/prompt, tools/network, delegation, context policy, exposed seed/temperature,
time/token bounds and grader identity. Requested and resolved values differ
explicitly; unavailable metadata has a reason. Configuration changes mark
incomparability. Record the observable native instruction/skill/tool surface
identity at first and resumed turns, its changes and unavailable portions.
Matched-comparison claims are qualified when those surfaces differ or remain
unknown; a valid upstream score retains its meaning. Prompt delivery alone
proves no native activation. No harness-offset subtraction is valid.

Each counted trial has an actual native launch, prompt, raw transcript, new
candidate artifact, observed exit/time/usage and independent grader observation,
linked to case, split, round, trial and benchmark revision. An agent_attempt
label alone proves nothing. Environment/permission/launcher failures are typed
exclusions under declared policy, not target failures; candidate failure in an
established environment remains an attempt. All exclusions have reasons;
missing/corrupt evidence, duplicate trials and controls-only records are defects.

Report per-case attempted/valid/pass/fail/invalid/unverified counts, failure
classes and uncertainty beside distinct-case and attempt totals. Functional
control separation does not establish empirical discrimination: inspect mixed
responses, failure diversity, repeatability and headroom. Optional configuration
comparisons remain separate, with paired cases and inversions; no second
configuration is required for reference calibration. Tiny panels justify no
item-total correlation. Record cost units, limits, estimates and actual spend;
unavailable tokens/prices stay unavailable.

## Calibration interpretation

The default inclusive pass@1 band is [0.30, 0.50], a user design choice, not
universal research law. Sampling declares case sets or independent generation,
splits, strata/case weights, trials, independent unit, order/seed, retry/exclusion
policy, revision/sample budgets and uncertainty rule before attempts. Pass@1
is the weighted mean of per-case first-attempt success probabilities estimated
from isolated trials, equally weighted within declared strata by default.
One trial per case gives the weighted case-pass fraction. Repeats estimate
fixed-case stochasticity, not extra independent cases; best-of-k is not pass@1.
Pass^k requires a supporting repeated-trial design.

Binomial/Wilson intervals require a defensible binomial unit and stated
assumptions; per-case repeated-trial intervals are conditional on that case.
Purposive heterogeneous suites give finite-suite means and per-case uncertainty,
with no population claim. Greater rigor may require predeclared case-cluster
bootstrap or independent cases; resampling repeats as independent cases is
invalid. Numeric IN_BAND and qualification for CALIBRATED are separate facts.

Decision precedence is INVALID for construct-defeating invalidity/contamination;
UNVERIFIED for missing required validity, coverage, configuration, sampling or
isolation evidence; otherwise OUT_OF_BAND outside the fixed band; CALIBRATED
only with validity, coverage, empirical diagnostics and declared uncertainty
satisfied. Required UNVERIFIED bars accepted validity and eligibility. Optional
gaps remain explicit. Numeric band observation survives higher-priority gaps.

## Revision and frozen evidence

Development revisions have predeclared bounds (default two), construct-preserving
reasons, pre/post identities, affected cases, preserved strata/weights, audit
identity, spend and complete sampling rounds. Genuine validity defects take
priority. Licensed compositional depth, horizon, explicit constraints or failure
classes may change difficulty; a score alone is insufficient justification.
Changed cases/shared graders need independent requalification. No configuration
or band substitution, selective retention, relabeled failures or flattering
rerun selection is valid. Every round remains attributable.

A frozen evaluation follows satisfied development policy and binds final
prompts/cases/seeds, keys/graders, split/weights, configuration, sample policy and
source mappings at one git revision before final attempts. Results are external
identities; scoring never rewrites frozen manifests. Final failure/drift is
recorded without in-place repair. Later changes create new versions/campaigns;
comparisons re-evaluate retained candidates in pairs across identity boundaries.
Saturation evidence supports a version proposal, not frozen mutation.

Protected evaluation uses evaluator-side checks after candidate output; candidate
workspace/input excludes protected evidence. Protection claims need actual
sandbox/ACL/container read controls and adversarial access observations across
history, caches, transcripts, fixtures, retrieval/network and scorer writes.
Sibling storage, omitted prompts, shared agent tools and native read-only writes
do not establish read exclusion. Without it, confirmation remains public or
development and final protection UNVERIFIED. Dated attacks distinguish SUCCEEDED,
FAILED and BLOCKED; failed bounded attacks prove no universal resistance.
Unrepaired holes name working attacks. Gaps and unexecuted branches are explicit.

Data layouts live in the package references; they grant no exemption from these
criteria. Legacy controls retain their original meaning and cannot imply
empirical calibration or calibrated eligibility.
