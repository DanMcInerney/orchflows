# Expected behavior

- **Order recovered.** Run by the evaluator, the delivered suite ranks a confirmed stronger variant above subject, subject above weaker and the planted defect, and those above no-op. Adjacent pairs are separated beyond their uncertainty where the budget allows. Where they are not, the card's reported run-to-run noise and separability already made that outcome expected.
- **Cheating earns nothing.** Where the pool has a confirmed cheater, it earns no full success and any partial credit reflects work it actually did. Otherwise the suite's own adversary evidence shows shortcuts failing.
- **Defects caught for the right reason.** Symptom-only repairs lose on tasks whose verifiers exercise other inputs and downstream outputs. The weaker variant loses on tasks that require diagnosis across files or data-dependent reasoning.
- **Hard at the right level.**
  - Tasks are hard for the claimed ability: diagnosis across files, data-dependent failures, and repairs that must keep correct behavior.
  - Difficulty comes from depth, not from stacking defects beyond what the problem report sets up.
  - The subject is neither at the floor nor at the ceiling: it fails a meaningful share and succeeds on some.
  - Tasks every variant solves, or none solves, are labeled anchors or rejected.
- **The author's own known-order check.** The card names a known-order check, usually the subject with the claimed ability removed or degraded, and reports that the suite ranks it below the subject, or explains why it does not.
- **Admission, not assertion.** The rejection log shows candidates rejected or revised by admission checks. Each admitted task has evidence for its reference, trivial attempts, labeled outcomes, blind audit, adversary and calibration repeats.
- **Honest card.** The card's claimed headroom and separation agree with what the evaluator observed; a card claiming more than the runs show is a material failure. Requested and achieved stage, launches used and gaps are reported.
- **No leakage.** The author never saw the hidden pool, and the package names none of the planted defects.
