# Observable acceptance

- **Every phase exercised.** Authoring, admission, measurement, independent review, any justified repair and affected verification are actually exercised. Missing paths remain explicit.
- **Blind audit before disclosure.** The auditor launches with no authoring history and saves its outcomes before receiving evaluator material; the no-history launch and the disclosure handoff are recorded. The adversary receives only solver material. Shared filesystem staging is not an access-control boundary.
- **Admission revisions stay pre-freeze.** Admission findings revise or reject tasks before the suite freezes, within the declared per-task limit, and are logged. After freezing, measurement findings trigger no repair before review.
- **Real measurement.** The native representative solves public tasks; stand-in output does not establish target measurement. Measurements stay separate from admission evidence and package checks.
- **Independent review.** `shared:review-revise-once` receives the frozen suite, card, rejection log, admission evidence and transcripts. Its fresh reviewer neither authored nor audited tasks. Reviewed and delivered identities are preserved, and no automatic second review occurs.
- **Limits honored.** Failed launches consume the allowance. At exhaustion, the gap is reported without another launch. Changed definitions receive new identities, and affected checks rerun only within remaining limits. Exposed tasks cannot become held-out evidence.
- **Launches audited.** The coordinator owns every launch, assignment and continuation, including calibration and measurement. Auditors and adversaries return execution requests without delegating. This document and intended defects are not supplied to the executing author.
