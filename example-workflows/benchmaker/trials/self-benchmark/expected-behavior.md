# Expected behavior

- **Verifiers execute, not inspect.** Each task's verifier runs the delivered benchmark's own commands against the hidden subject pool. It computes order recovery, defect detection and cheater credit from those runs. It never treats the delivered card, file layout or field names as evidence of quality.
- **Builder pool ranked correctly.** Run by the evaluator against the hidden builder pool, the delivered suite scores full Benchmaker above Benchmaker without admission and the plain agent. The fabricated and empty packages earn nothing. Where one attempt per builder cannot separate them, the author's card already says the suite would need more repeats or tasks.
- **Subject pools with a checked order.** Pools span at least two kinds of subject. Planted defects lower real ability and are grounded in documented agent or benchmark failures. The pool's order is confirmed by a direct check before admission. Pools and their order stay outside every builder's workspace.
- **Self-evaluation risk handled.** The same library authors the tasks and is a builder under test, so the risk is named. Defects are not derived from Benchmaker's own guidance or admission criteria. Where feasible, a different model family or an author without that guidance builds the subject pools; otherwise the gap is recorded.
- **Admission of the meta-tasks.**
  - A known-good reference benchmark for each subject passes.
  - An empty package fails.
  - An adversarial package that fakes its card or results fails.
  - A blind auditor finds every graded requirement stated in the task.
- **Real execution, honest accounting.** Builders run as native workflows or agents in separate top-level sessions. Builder sessions and subject runs are counted against their separate limits. One execution per builder yields a draft: no difficulty, discrimination or reliability claim follows from one attempt.
- **Honest delivery.** The card, rejection log, costs and time are reported. The next unmet stage names the repeats and tasks needed. Every gap is named, including sessions refused by the host.
