# Expected behavior

- **Hard, distinct tasks.** Candidates exceed the admitted suite. Admitted tasks differ in required work and cannot be answered from any single document. Each carries a difficulty rationale and an expert time estimate. Source-group splits suit a bounded offline claim.
- **Checkable citation support.** Claims are traced to quoted corpus passages; reference answers are verified against the corpus in full. A fluent unsupported report fails evidence criteria, and a different supported synthesis passes.
- **Admission evidence.** The adversary's attempts to earn credit, such as fabricated or mismatched citations, confident summaries of one source or enumerated alternatives, fail. The auditor's saved outcomes precede disclosure.
- **Provisional judging.** Semantic quality without a calibrated judge stays provisional, and writing quality never erases unsupported claims.
- **Real execution.** The supplied workflow is invoked on fresh inputs, never replaced by a direct model call. Delivered reports, conditions and traces are retained.
- **Comparison set.** The workflow is compared with a simple baseline at matched budget, such as the same model answering alone with the corpus, or the baseline's absence is explained.
- **Honest delivery.** The card reports headroom, discrimination, the rejection log, the 30-execution limit, coverage, latency and cost. A time target cannot justify hidden truncation.
