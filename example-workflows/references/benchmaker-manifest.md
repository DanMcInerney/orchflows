# Benchmark manifest compatibility pointer

The package-local [manifest dictionary](../benchmaker/references/manifest.md)
owns fields and legacy/v2 interpretation; the
[calibration record dictionary](../benchmaker/references/calibration-record.md)
owns empirical attempt and summary fields.

This retained path adds no mandatory guidance. Existing v1 fixture and
historical measurement identities remain readable as legacy evidence.
They are not implicitly empirical calibration or campaign eligibility.
