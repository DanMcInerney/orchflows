# Benchmaker protocol compatibility pointer

Current benchmark quality is owned by the package-private
[benchmark-quality standard](../benchmaker/standards/benchmark-quality/STANDARD.md).
The [public workflow](../benchmaker/SKILL.md) owns invocation and the
[manifest dictionary](../benchmaker/references/manifest.md) owns data fields.
This retained path adds no mandatory guidance. Historical corpus/measurement
identities retain their original meaning; their controls do not establish
empirical target calibration.
