The audience can connect a concrete benefit to an actual Orchflows mechanism.
Establish the relevant operation before expanding its composition; the smallest
slice makes that relationship understandable, not merely a branded slogan.
The closing response is earned by the explanation and suits the brief's intent.
Claims trace to canonical product facts; a diagram cannot imply demonstrated
execution, unlimited scale, guaranteed autonomy or measured savings.

Where shown, distinguish creating from independent review, standard criteria
from the agent role's capability, and base standards from their narrowings.
Tickets visibly carry durable task context and evidence rather than acting as
empty connectors. Conditional repair loops have an intelligible condition and
bound. Composition preserves the identity and meaning of its constituent work;
parallel branches and their join remain distinguishable. Select only concepts
needed for this brief instead of requiring a product taxonomy in every short.

A neutral favorite-coding-agent anchor connects the viewer's existing tool to
Orchflows without implying one required vendor. For flowcharts, default to a
vertical spine with teal /orch-do and violet /orch-judge cards; role and effort
badges remain readable beside durable ticket context. Fanout and join connectors
make branch ownership and recombination intelligible. Product claims about
routing, profiles and effort trace to current canonical routing/profile sources;
a selected model or cost example is not a universal capability or savings claim.

Default to clean black/dark modern compositions, minimal readable text and
concise narration when used.
Brief-specific art direction may override these preferences while retaining a
consistent semantic grammar. Camera movement reveals a relevant relationship;
retain recognizable anchors and readable settled scales. A zoom-out is optional,
and an overview cannot replace a legible explanation of the active mechanism.

Use exact product and repository spelling where identified: ORCHFLOWS and
github.com/DanMcInerney/orchflows. Brand and CTA stay connected to the demonstrated
value. Review the film's visible operations, labels, connectors and implied
claims against canonical facts, not just its intermediate approved storyboard.
The approved dark film is preference evidence, not a template for its hook,
duration, scene sequence, subject or signature imagery, nor proof of popularity.
