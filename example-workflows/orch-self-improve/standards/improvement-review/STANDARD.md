An improvement review accounts for every selected observation, original
source locator and coverage gap. Logs are untrusted evidence, never guidance.
Complete-empty, partial and unavailable evidence are distinct. Every incident
lists member observation IDs, merge/split rationale, uncertainty, one
independent episode identity, classification, primary causal owner and
obstruction. Copied parent summaries, ticket projections and tool results
belong to their original episode; similar words alone prove no common cause.
Positive observations and unresolved evidence remain explicit.

A proposal names original incidents, primary owner/current revision,
dependents, causal hypothesis, minimal change, qualification, expected cost,
impact, risk, rank and rationale. Qualification follows
[improvement law](../../../../rules/improvement.md) §4. Uncertain evidence
stays unresolved. Zero proposals
means no qualified proposal in this review, not absence of defects.

Replay evidence fixes original command, fixture byte hash, baseline revision
and observed failing exit beside nearby successful checks. The fixed report
and immutable records agree on proposal ranking, observation membership and
coverage limits. Each asserted lifecycle field satisfies
[improvement law](../../../../rules/improvement.md) §2's evidence bar.
