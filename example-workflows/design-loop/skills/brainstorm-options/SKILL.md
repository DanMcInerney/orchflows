Use the [handoff contract](../../references/design-loop-contract.md). Inputs: request context, identified baseline and any prior decisions, tests or analysis. Omitted standalone history means a first increment.

For assignment `brainstorm-options`, propose a few distinct increments with changed behavior, endgoal relevance, scope, observable benefit, risks and research questions. Later options respond to observations, including failed or retained work. Return a tentative preference and assumptions; do not implement.
