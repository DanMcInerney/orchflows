# Site evidence handoff

Use compact Markdown, normally `results.md` in a unique worker directory. This is a shared reading convention, not a machine schema. Compatible saved evidence can be judged without rerunning acquisition.

Identify the site and resolved scope: question, source/window restrictions, information cutoff or forecast horizon when relevant, required depth and allocated bounds. Name the access method separately from the source origin, including any authorized search index or archive.

State one outcome:

- `complete`: the bounded assignment and its required details are supported; this does not imply exhaustive site coverage.
- `partial`: useful evidence exists, but required details, coverage or acquisition remain incomplete.
- `blocked`: a constraint, refusal or failure prevents a usable result for the assignment; preserve any evidence already acquired.
- `no_results`: permitted reads completed and found no matching candidates in the searched scope. Describe that scope and its limits; this is not proof that the site contains none.

A missing or failed worker is not `no_results`. Identify empty discovery, unavailable access, incomplete depth and unmeasured window reach separately so a judge can assess their effect. A claimed complete result with a material required gap must be treated as partial.

Provide a locally ranked list. Each candidate carries:

- A stable site-prefixed ID, retained across follow-ups, and the original URL and title exactly as observed. If no title is supplied, mark any descriptive replacement as your own. Include the author/account when relevant and known.
- Content/publication and event dates where relevant, unknowns explicit, plus the actual observation timestamp with timezone. Neither a publication date nor a current engagement count establishes a past observation.
- Supporting excerpts, accurately labeled paraphrases, or accessible saved source-response material with a precise locator. Supply enough context for the judge to check the proposed claim without repeating the search. Label search snippets as snippets; a source link, worker assertion or opaque tool handle unavailable to the judge is insufficient by itself. Respect quotation limits.
- A relevance/rank reason and material limitations, such as inaccessible primary evidence, missing transcript segments, archive lag, differing horizons or uncertain dates. Keep source assertions distinguishable from verified facts.

Platform engagement is optional context attached to its own record and observation time. A comment retains its own author and count; never substitute a parent's score. Unknown counts stay unknown. Scores, local ordinal ranks and repeated posts cannot become cross-site credibility scores.

Finish with compact coverage and usage notes: what was searched/read, selection and depth omissions that affect the question, required-source/detail gaps, window reach, refusals, caps reached, measured usage and unmeasured dimensions. State elapsed time or actual start/end times when available; do not reconstruct a manual tool ledger. Preserve already available raw responses, receipts and backend packet paths/digests in their existing files, linking them where useful rather than duplicating them into another metadata bundle. Declare typed backend losses without obscuring their practical effect.
