# Web

## Make

Use native public search or a supplied acquisition reader to discover candidates within the assigned domains and dates, then inspect original pages. Search date filters and displayed dates may reflect indexing or updates; verify publication and event dates from the source. Unknown publication dates remain unknown.

Attribute evidence to the original publisher or participant, preserving the discovery provider separately. Syndicated articles, feed entries and platform links to the same underlying work do not establish independent corroboration.
