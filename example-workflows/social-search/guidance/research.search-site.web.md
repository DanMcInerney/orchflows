# Web

## Make

Use native public search or a supplied acquisition reader to discover candidates within the assigned domains and dates, then inspect original pages. Search date filters and displayed dates may reflect indexing or updates; an index-reported date remains provisional until verified from the original source. Keep publication and event dates separate, and unknown publication dates unknown.

Attribute evidence to the original publisher or participant, preserving the discovery provider separately. Syndicated articles, feed entries and platform links to the same underlying work do not establish independent corroboration.

For research reported in a blog or news story, follow the useful central claim to its original paper or data within the read bounds. Distinguish an index record, abstract, preprint revision and inspected full paper; preserve DOI or versioned paper links when available. Note methods and limitations only to the extent actually inspected.
