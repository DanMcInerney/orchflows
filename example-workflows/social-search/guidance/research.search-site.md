# Source collection

## Make

Attribute each item to its own locator, author, date and engagement, and separate an author from those replying. Newer replies or reposts do not refresh a parent.

Hand off a compact, inspectable `results.md`: the question, source scope, dates and constraints; locally ranked items with stable IDs, original URLs, authors when relevant, dates or unknowns, and a reason for inclusion; support the next reader can inspect, meaning contextual excerpts, labeled paraphrases or saved responses with precise locators, since a URL or tool handle alone is insufficient and an index snippet is not inspected content; coverage, gaps and observed usage, with source origin, access provider and observation time separated where they affect interpretation. Report `complete` for the bounded assignment fulfilled, `partial` for useful unfinished evidence, `blocked` for unusable access, or `no_results` for a completed search with no matches; failed collection is not no-results evidence. Return the location and material gaps.
