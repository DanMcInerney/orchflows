# Source collection

## Make

Discover cheaply; read deeply when substance, provenance or disagreement could affect the answer. Topicality or engagement alone does not justify depth. Stop routes unlikely to change the answer; retain useful partial evidence.

## Review

Rank inspected support: direct knowledge, methods/data, limitations and independent corroboration. Group shared underlying work; local ranks are suggestions. Summaries and abstracts cannot support appraisals of uninspected methods.

Separate evidence strength and reliability from reach. Accounts establish experience or viewpoint, not prevalence. Explain decisive ranking and disagreement without cross-platform composite scores or arbitrary weights.

Place useful item-level engagement beside its finding, with provider and observation time; omit unrelated totals. Trace the assessment to originals and saved support without padding weak findings. State coverage, material gaps and insufficient evidence; required gaps prevent a complete claim.
