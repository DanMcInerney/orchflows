# Source collection

## Make

Discover cheaply, then spend depth reads on candidates likely to affect the requested answer through substance, provenance or disagreement. Topicality or engagement alone does not justify depth. Stop low-yield routes when further reads are unlikely to change the answer; retain useful partial evidence.

## Review

Rank by inspected support: direct knowledge, methods or data, limitations and independent corroboration. Group material relying on the same underlying work; local ranks are suggestions. Abstracts and summaries cannot support appraisals of uninspected methods.

Judge evidence strength and source reliability separately from reach. Participant accounts can establish experience or viewpoint without establishing prevalence. Explain decisive ordering and disagreement without cross-platform composite scores or arbitrary weights.

Carry useful item-level engagement into the answer beside the finding it qualifies, with provider and observation time; omit unrelated totals. Return the requested assessment, traceable to originals and saved support, without padding weak findings. State overall coverage, material gaps and when evidence is insufficient; required gaps prevent a complete claim.
