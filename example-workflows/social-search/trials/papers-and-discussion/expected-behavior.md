# Trial acceptance

Apply the [shared acceptance](../expected-behavior.md), plus:

- Paper/blog overlap informs assignment ownership; community collectors contribute participant evidence without repeating shared original-paper depth reads.
- The six-minute total leaves time for the final assessment. Low-yield routes stop with useful partial evidence and gaps retained.
- Abstracts, full papers, blog summaries and participant claims remain distinguishable. Publication, revision and event dates do not substitute for each other.
- The final answer retains relevant item-level engagement with provenance when available, separately from evidence strength. It includes no aggregate popularity score or unrelated source totals.
- Inspect whether low-engagement originals can outrank weak viral claims when both occur. If either is absent, record the unexercised comparison. Fewer than four findings or insufficient evidence is acceptable.
