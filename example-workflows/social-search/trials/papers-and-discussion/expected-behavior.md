# Trial acceptance

Inspect actual handoffs, the final report and agent events. Record candidate versions, resolved paths, tools, elapsed time and interventions; do not credit an unexercised behavior.

- Exactly three skills remain available. Useful source scopes fit at most two collection workers; closely related paper/blog sources can share an assignment. One fresh reviewer receives all outcomes and the shared source Review criteria. It collects no additional material.
- The target budget and read deadline survive delegation. Discovery advances to depth for a substantive reason; low-yield or blocked routes stop, with useful partial evidence and gaps retained.
- Included claims identify the content actually inspected and lead back to original research where useful. Abstracts, full papers, blog summaries and participant claims stay distinguishable. Publication, revision and event dates do not substitute for each other; in-window evidence, older context and undated leads stay distinct.
- The handoff preserves available native counts and observation times, missing metrics versus observed zeros, and sampled comments with parent context. Visibility and sentiment claims do not exceed those observations.
- The assessment explains quality and reliability separately from reach. A useful low-engagement primary paper can outrank unsupported viral or low-signal claims; firsthand opinions remain evidence of the reported experience. The ranking uses no cross-platform composite score, arbitrary weights or engagement threshold.
- Inspect that comparison in the actual evidence when available; if one side is absent, record the test coverage gap rather than inventing engagement or a weak comparison item. Fewer than four supported findings is acceptable. An insufficient-evidence conclusion is preferable to padding.
- Artifacts remain outside the packages, with a handoff per assignment and a report that accurately states complete, partial or blocked coverage.
