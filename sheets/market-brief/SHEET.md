# market-brief

## Making

The brief serves one decision: whether to enter, price against, or leave a
market. Everything below narrows the research craft toward that decision;
nothing here relaxes it.

- **One audience, named.** Say who buys, at what size and in what role,
  and what they use today. An audience given as a category word is not an
  audience; a brief that cannot name one is a gap, not a brief.
- **Pains, shown rather than inferred.** Every pain carries a dated
  quotation from a member of that audience, with its author and its
  engagement count where the surface reports one. A pain assembled out of
  a vendor's marketing is that vendor's claim about a pain, and is
  labelled as such.
- **Three or more competitors, each with its own hook.** The hook is the
  promise the competitor leads with, quoted from its own surface, with the
  price it publishes where it publishes one and the one pain in this
  brief's list it does not serve. A review site's summary is a secondary
  reading of a hook, never the hook.
- **Trends that are live and measured.** Each trend is a movement inside
  the window with a magnitude, a unit and two dates. A direction word with
  no figure under it is an impression.
- **The window is named once, at the top, with the `as_of` the reads were
  taken at.** Every claim is dated. A claim whose date falls outside the
  window is dropped rather than carried as background.
- **Gaps are part of the brief.** Audience, pains, competitors and trends
  each say what the admitted sources did not answer. A thin section
  declared beats a full one inferred.

## Lens

### evidence

- Audience: named at size and role, and the evidence that this audience is
  the buyer rather than the user is present.
- Pains: each carries a dated, verbatim quotation with its author and its
  count; a pain whose only support is a vendor's own copy is labelled or
  it is a finding.
- Competitors: at least three, each hook quoted from that competitor's own
  surface with its date; a hook resting on a secondary summary is a
  finding, and so is a fourth competitor added without one.
- Trends: each carries a magnitude, a unit and two dates inside the window.
- Window: named once with its `as_of`; every claim dated; no claim dated
  outside it.
- Gaps: declared per section - then look for the gaps the declaration
  missed, which is the reading this lens exists to take.
