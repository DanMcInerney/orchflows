The artifact preserves the request's landing scope and names its authoring
owner in governed Context. Apply the
[authoring lens](../../docs/custom-workflow-authoring.md#authoring-lens).

Its concise contract inventory explains reuse choices and each new boundary's
actual callers or independent journal reason. The smallest useful slice
crosses a meaningful public seam; layer count and wrappers are not evidence.
Control flow, applied methods, and quality guidance have the separate owners
in [composition](../../rules/composition.md). A public call supplies
semantic inputs and resolves its standards in the callee's scope. Narrowing
guidance adds a recurring quality constraint without copying or weakening its
base, under the [standard contract](../../contracts/standard.md).

Evidence distinguishes the fixed package, generated adapters, static admission,
and actual disposable-project invocation; admission not yet observed is an
explicit gap, including when a later caller owns it. Runtime observations
name run/frame/ticket identities, standard pins shared by maker and judge,
landed artifacts, independent findings, and external probe exit codes. An
output probe has an observed failing reading for absent or corrupt output and
a passing reading for the actual result. Unexecuted branches and unavailable
tools remain explicit gaps; static success cannot prove prose executed.

Review defects include duplicated orchestration where a suitable contract
exists, a boundary without a consumer, a private name lost at a public call,
scope expansion, unpinned evidence, and a success claim beyond observed
admission. Existing failure and partial-result contracts retain their force.
