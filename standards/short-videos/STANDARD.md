## Outline and slice

Fix audience, intended understanding or action, duration, placement, delivery
settings, voice/citation constraints and permitted assets/providers. The opening
gives a concrete reason to attend; the payoff fulfills that promise. Support
subject claims and distinguish illustration from witnessed results. Exposed
counts retain dates and sampling limits; they prove no causal effectiveness.

The smallest useful slice pairs one idea with its visible explanation. Each beat
changes understanding, with a before/after state, focal subject and continuity
across its joins. Budget the whole duration, including settled reading holds and
intentional overlaps. One readable idea may fill one second; longer work earns
its time through explanation and relief, not stretched cards or a fixed cut quota.
Narration and reading estimates remain assumptions until measured.

Direction specifies timed wording, visual action, transition purpose, captions,
sound and assets (explicit none where omitted), plus claim support, rights,
research-to-choice reasoning and production gaps. Original wording and treatment
must not copy a reference's signature expression or imply asset permission.
Dated observations identify inspected positions and limits; unsupported creative
choices are design hypotheses. Direction acceptance proves no rendered quality.

Illustrated storyboards show the intended composition, not only a text outline.
Panels cover important visual states and joins with enough views to explain the
film; no fixed panel count applies. Each identifies timing, visible action,
narration or explicit silence, focal point and transition purpose. Essential
text, badges and relationships remain legible at the target display size;
inspect dense panels at that scale, not only enlarged on an authoring canvas.
Static direction evidence establishes layout and planned continuity, never
motion, measured speech timing or heard quality.

## Realization

Hierarchy, concise type, contrast against the moving background and placement-
specific safe areas keep essential information readable at target display size.
Inspect final fonts and widest/densest text; clipping, caption competition or a
payoff readable only while paused is a defect. Meaningful visual demonstration
should replace redundant text when it explains a process or relationship better.

Motion directs attention and preserves tracked objects and spatial relationships.
Entrances, easing, camera changes and transitions serve the before/after relation;
ornamental movement that hides the event, unexplained teleportation and transitions
that consume the payoff's reading time are defects. Recurring visual cues retain
meaning. Render timing is deterministic and accounts for overlaps and final holds.

When used, narration adds explanation without competing with dense reading;
captions preserve its words and measured phrase timing, pauses and emphasis.
Word highlighting requires qualified alignment, not evenly divided phrase times.
Music and effects support emphasis without masking voice or truncating endings.
An explicitly silent brief communicates visually; it cannot hide missing narration.
Asset, font, music and voice provenance includes permissions and immutable identities;
evaluation eligibility does not establish production licensing.

## Review and delivery

Bind evidence to the source commit and exported file hash. Compare the actual
encoded film with the original brief and accepted direction at normal speed and
target scale across its full duration, including joins and first/last frames.
Record player, timestamps, dense/moving samples and comprehension/tempo defects.
Stills, contact sheets and metadata supplement playback; they cannot prove motion.

Compare visible captions with the script and final speech at identified positions.
A listening-capable reviewer hears the exact mix for pronunciation, intelligibility,
completeness, musical fit and perceived sync. Transcript, signal and timing checks
do not prove hearing. Unobserved modalities remain explicit unresolved criteria
with a playable audition where applicable; human verdicts identify listener, file
and coverage. Repair evidence identifies the responsible beat or join.

Delivery defaults to 1080x1920, 30fps, H264/yuv420p, limited-range BT.709 primaries,
transfer and matrix; required audio uses AAC 48kHz, -16 LUFS +/-2 and true peak
at most -1 dBTP. Brief-approved alternatives are explicit. Duration matches within
one frame; decode the entire export. Absent, corrupt, wrong-size or wrong-duration
output is a defect. Optional silence does not waive targets for present audio.

Retain locked runnable source, restoration/render/preview instructions, final MP4,
asset/timing provenance, caption timeline and cover/contact sheet, plus audition
when applicable. Large uncommitted assets need immutable retrievable identities
and verified restoration. Evidence names probe settings/exits, direct observations
and uncovered behavior; a short technical fixture proves no unproduced duration
or creative branch.
